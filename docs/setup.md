# Initial setup

Open **Financial Hub** and choose **Settings**. You'll work through a few tabs. You can change any of this later.

## General

On the **General** tab, fill in:

- **Campaign Name**.
- **Election Date**. The dashboard uses it to project your balance for Election Day.
- **Overall Fundraising Goal**. This drives the progress bar on the dashboard.
- **Beginning Balance**. The money you had in the bank when you started tracking in the Hub.
- **Filing State**. Choose **Tennessee** if you file there. It's the only state with a report export today.
- **Unitemized Threshold**. The Auto-Allocate tool uses it: gifts below this amount can be marked unitemized, meaning they're reported as a total instead of one by one. Tennessee uses $100.
- **My reports don't use elections (PAC or party)**. Tick this if your reports aren't split by election. It hides the election fields and the contribution limits.

Choose **Save** when you're done.

## Filing periods

On the **Filing Periods** tab, add one period for each report you file, with a name, its **Election Type**, and its start and end dates. The election type is required: pick Primary, General, Runoff, Special, or Local. New gifts dated in the period take that election automatically. The Hub sorts every donation and expense into the period its date falls in.

## Budget categories

On the **Budget Categories** tab, add the categories you want to sort expenses into, such as printing, postage, and advertising.

## Contribution limits

On the **Contribution Limits** tab, set the most one donor may give in the **Primary**, the **General**, and a **Runoff**. The **Near-Limit Warning (%)** controls how close to a limit a donor gets before the Hub flags them. If your rules set no limits, tick **No contribution limits**. Organizations have their own limits on the same tab.
