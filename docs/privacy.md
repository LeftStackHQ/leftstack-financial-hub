# Privacy and donor records

The Hub stores donors' names, addresses, employers, emails, and phone numbers, and bank statement lines. It plugs into WordPress's own privacy tools so you can answer a person who asks what you hold about them.

## Find what you hold about a person

Go to **Tools**, then **Export Personal Data**, enter the person's email address, and send the request. The export includes:

- **Financial Hub donor records:** the date, amount, and payment method of their contributions, and the name, address, occupation, employer, phone, and email you recorded.
- **Financial Hub bank lines:** the date, amount, and description of any bank statement line that names them.

Staff notes are left out of the export. They are your campaign's working notes, not what the donor gave.

## Erase requests

**Tools**, then **Erase Personal Data**, works the same way, but the Hub deletes nothing. Contribution records and bank lines are campaign financial records. Your state requires you to keep them for audits and to report them, so the eraser keeps them and tells the person why.

If you want to remove a contribution because it was entered in error, delete it in the Hub as usual. That is a bookkeeping correction, not a privacy request.

## Who can do this

WordPress decides, not the Hub. Only administrators can use the export and erase tools (on a network, super admins). The Hub's own staff role can't, so a person who works in the Hub can't pull a donor's records out this way.

## Privacy policy text

The Hub adds suggested text to your site's privacy policy guide, under **Settings**, then **Privacy**. It describes the contribution and bank line records you keep. Read it, edit it to fit your campaign and your state, and add it to your policy page.

!!! note "A legal reminder"
    This page describes what the plugin does. It isn't legal advice, and privacy rules differ by state. Ask your campaign's attorney what to put in your policy and how long you must keep records.
