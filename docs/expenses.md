# Enter an expense

Use this for every payment the campaign makes: printing, postage, ads, fees, and so on.

1. Go to **Expenditures** and choose **Add Expense**.
2. Fill in the form, then choose **Add Expense** again to save.

## What's in the form

**Payee:** the **Payee** name is required. As you type, the Hub suggests payees you've used before and fills in their address. **Address** is optional.

**Dates:**

- **Expense Date** is the day you paid or wrote the check. It decides which filing period the expense lands in.
- **Date Posted** is the day the money left your bank account. Leave it empty until it clears. If the expense date is in the future, this field stays locked until that day arrives.

**Amount and payment:**

- **Amount** is required.
- **Payment Method** is Check, Debit, Credit, Cash, ACH, Transfer, Wire, Fee, or Other. Choosing Check shows a **Check #** box.
- **Category** sorts the expense into one of your [budget categories](setup.md). Leave it as **Uncategorized** if none fits.
- **Description** is what you bought. Write enough that a reader of your report understands the purpose.

**Receipt:** upload a JPEG, PNG, or PDF, or paste a link to one (a Google Drive or Dropbox link works). Receipts are optional, but keep them.

**Other:** **Paid By** is for expenses someone on the team paid out of pocket. **Filing Period** fills in from the expense date. **Balanced to Bank Statement** is ticked once you've checked it against the bank; the [bank matching](bank.md) can tick it for you. **Notes** are for your team and don't appear in your filed report.

!!! tip "Write a real purpose"
    Many states reject vague descriptions such as "reimbursement" or "campaign expense". Tennessee does. Say what the money bought and for whom.

## Keep track of them

The **Transactions** tab lists every expense. You can search, filter by category, method, status (**Scheduled**, **Pending**, or **Posted**) or period, and tick several rows to change their category, method, or Balanced setting at once. **Import** reads expenses from a CSV, and **Export** downloads the rows you're looking at.

The other tabs:

- **Payees** shows what you've paid each payee in total, and the categories they fall under.
- **Budget** compares each category's budget with what you've spent and what's left.
- **Vendors** compares what you spent with a vendor against the money raised through their campaigns. You group payees and fundraising pages together, and the Hub shows spending, money raised, refunds, fees, and the cost per dollar raised.
