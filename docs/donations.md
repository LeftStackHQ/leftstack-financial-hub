# Enter a donation

Use this for checks, cash, and anything else that didn't come through ActBlue.

1. Go to **Donations** and choose **+ Add Donation** at the top right.
2. Fill in the form, then save.

## What's in the form

**Donor Information:** first and last name, address, city, state, ZIP, occupation, employer, email, and phone. Your state's rules decide which of these you must collect for gifts you itemize.

**Amount & Dates:**

- **Gross Amount** is required. It's the full amount the donor gave.
- **Fee** is any processing fee taken out of it.
- **Contribution Date** is the day the donor gave. This decides which filing period the gift lands in.
- **Date Deposited** is the day it reached your bank account.

**Election, report type, and status:**

- **Election** is the election the gift is for. It fills in from the filing period the gift's date falls in, and you can change it.
- **Report Type** is required. **Itemized** gifts are listed one by one in your report. **Unitemized** gifts are reported as a total. Your state's rules decide which is which; in Tennessee, gifts over $100 are itemized.
- **Pending** means the gift hasn't been reported yet. It becomes **Filed** after you file.

Use **Notes** for anything you want to remember. Notes are for your team and don't appear in your filed report.

!!! tip "Lots of gifts to enter?"
    If you have them in a spreadsheet, use **Import CSV** on the **Disbursements** tab instead. It reads most column layouts and lets you match the columns before anything is saved.
