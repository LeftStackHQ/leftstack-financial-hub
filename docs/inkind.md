# Record an in-kind contribution

An in-kind contribution is goods or services given to the campaign free, or for less than they're worth. A donated venue, printing at a discount, and volunteer work from a business all count. Your state's rules say which of these you must report.

1. Go to **In-Kind** and choose **Add In-Kind**.
2. Fill in the form and save.

## What's in the form

**Donor:** name, address, occupation, and employer of the person or organization that gave.

**Contribution:**

- **Fair Market Value** is what the goods or services would have cost at normal prices. That is the number you report, not what the donor paid.
- **Date** is the day the campaign received them.
- **Description of goods/services** says what was given. Be specific, for example "Use of hall for the volunteer kickoff, 4 hours".
- **FEC Report Date** is optional. Fill it in only if your reports use it.
- **Filing Period** fills in from the date.
- **Notes** are for your team.

## Good to know

- The dashboard adds in-kind gifts into its own **in-kind total**, separate from cash donations.
- The **State Report** file on the Donations page doesn't include in-kind contributions. Use **Export** on the In-Kind page to download them as a CSV, and enter them in your state's report the way your state asks.
- The Hub's contribution limit warnings look at cash donations only. If a donor also gave in-kind, add the two together yourself when you check their limit.
- On the list you can search, filter by period, choose which columns show, and use **Import** to bring in a CSV.
