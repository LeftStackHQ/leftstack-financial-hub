# Before you file

Run this check before every report. It looks at one filing period and tells you which contributions are missing something your state needs, so you can fix them while you still have time.

1. Go to **Donations** and open the **Before you file** tab.
2. Pick the **Filing period**. It starts on the period that covers today.

The Hub looks at the itemized contributions that would go in the state report for that period and tells you how many it checked and how many are missing something. Nothing is changed by the check itself.

## Fix what's missing

Below the summary is a table of donors with a **Donor**, **Gifts**, **Total**, and **Missing** column. **Missing** lists what's absent: name, street, city, state, ZIP, and, for an individual, occupation or employer.

Choose **Edit** on a donor to open the contribution in the usual form, fix it, and save. The list updates.

Some donors never give an occupation or employer, however many times you ask. For them, choose **Best efforts**, enter the date under **Asked on**, add a **Note** such as "emailed twice", and save. That excuses the donor's occupation and employer from the check. They move to **Best efforts on file**, which lists who you asked, when, and how much they gave in that period. Choose **Remove** if you added one by mistake.

!!! tip "Keep your own proof"
    The Hub records that you asked, but it can't prove you did. Keep the email, letter, or text you sent.

## Compare with the state

If your state shows the list of contributions it has received for a period, you can check your records against it. In **Compare with the state**:

1. Save the state's list as a CSV (or tab separated file).
2. Choose it under **The state's list**.
3. Check which column holds the date, the amount, and the name, then choose **Compare**.

The Hub shows what is missing, extra, or different. It changes nothing. Organizations are matched by date, amount, and ZIP code, because the state keeps its own names for them.

## Refunds in a period you already filed

At the bottom of the tab, **Periods that need an amendment** lists refunds of donations from a period you've already filed. A donation is always reported in its own period, so when one is refunded after that period was filed, you amend that period on the state site. Then choose **Mark reported** here. The Hub sends nothing to the state. If a refund isn't linked to its original gift yet, choose **Link the original**.
